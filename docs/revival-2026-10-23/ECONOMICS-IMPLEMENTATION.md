## Computation
Operating: all entered fixed costs include owner pay; contribution margin is after variable costs. Fit-out recovery is explicit over a positive number of months. Report break-even revenue and transactions/day, affordable rent at entered sales and target surplus, low/base/high cases from entered assumptions, and capital exposure. Field-checked legal use and access/utilities remain hard conditions; demand remains unknown until measured. Industrial scenarios use orders/day and working-capital needs rather than assumed pedestrian demand.
Buy to let: annual potential rent minus vacancy/credit loss and operating costs including property tax = NOI. Financing and owner income tax separately derive annual cash flow. Acquisition all-in includes initial costs/fit-out; yield denominator is all-in acquisition. No appreciation assumption or arbitrary “good yield” default.
Comparables: identified, dated sources, matching space type/location/lease and usable size; rental prices separated by asking/quoted/agreed type. Deduplicate canonical source. Minimum five eligible observations for sample median/middle-half range; label sample comparison, not a market valuation. Suppress when stale, mixed or sparse. Historical benchmark unavailable without comparable history.
