# Next evidence needed for a location recommendation

The present release supports checking an actual quoted space and comparing six station-area anchors. It does not establish which Bangkok streets have unmet commercial demand.

The next bounded product test is one business activity in one catchment, with several real candidate premises. Each candidate needs an address/pin, usable area, permitted use/lease evidence, asking/quoted/agreed rent and included charges, observation date, entrance-to-transit route and accessibility checks. Compare premises, not only station names.

Customer demand must be measured separately from supply: observed pedestrian counts by weekday/time/weather, relevant customer segments, trade area and delivery radius, comparable operators and vacancy/turnover observations. A school or sportsground catalog record is neither customer volume nor spending power. Company registrations and transit system totals cannot supply storefront footfall.

For a live-work household decision, retain the actual journey destination/mode/departure, rent plus utilities/transport, car dependence and verified everyday needs. A straight-line circle is a search boundary, not a walking catchment.

A useful output is a short list of conditions and tradeoffs: which premises meet hard requirements; what rent the entered operating scenario can support; how much sales must fall before cash runs out; which assumptions remain unverified; and the next site visit needed. Suppress any candidate ranking whose evidence gaps could reverse it. No universal area score or investment heatmap.

History requires repeated comparable units or stable strata and a named baseline period. Until then, “reasonable compared with normal times” remains unanswered.
